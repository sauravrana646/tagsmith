# Data processing addendum (DPA)

**Draft. Last updated: 13 August 2026.**  
**Not legal advice.** For EEA/UK customers (and other customers who need a
processor contract). Execute as an addendum to the
[Terms](terms-of-service.md). Fill parties, annexes, and SCCs **before**
use. This is a **starting outline**, not a finished DPA.

## Parties

- **Customer** (controller): the Tagsmith account holder  
- **Provider** (processor): `[LEGAL_NAME]`, `[POSTAL_ADDRESS]`,
  `[PRIVACY_EMAIL]`

## 1. Subject

Provider processes personal data, including **Gmail-derived** data, only to
provide Tagsmith (classify and label the Customer’s mailbox) as described in
the [Privacy policy](privacy-policy.md).

## 2. Instructions

Provider processes only on documented instructions from Customer (use of the
service + these Terms/DPA), unless EU/UK/Indian law requires otherwise.

## 3. Confidentiality

Persons authorized to process data are bound to confidentiality.

## 4. Security

Reasonable measures: TLS in transit; encryption of tokens at rest; tenant
isolation; access control; no long-term storage of full bodies (excerpt TTL
7–14 days).

## 5. Sub-processors

Customer authorizes the vendors on [Sub-processors](sub-processors.md).
Provider will impose equivalent data-protection terms. Material additions:
notice via that page and, where required, an objection window.

## 6. International transfers

If Provider or a sub-processor processes EEA/UK data outside those regions,
Provider will use a valid mechanism (e.g. **SCCs** + UK addendum).  
**Annex to attach before signature:** the actual SCC modules and vendor
DPA links (`[LLM_PROCESSOR]`, `[HOSTING_PROCESSOR]`).

## 7. Assistance

Provider will assist Customer, insofar as possible, with data-subject
requests, DPIAs, and breach notices. Customer-facing deletion/export is
available in the product (disconnect, delete, label-history export).

## 8. Breach

Provider will notify Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of a
personal data breach affecting Customer data, with facts then known.

## 9. Deletion / return

On termination or Customer request, Provider will delete Gmail-derived
hosted data and tokens, except data we must keep for tax/law (billing
records). Backups expire with backup TTL.

## 10. Audits

On reasonable written notice, Provider will make available information
reasonably necessary to demonstrate this DPA (security overview, this
policy set). On-site audits only if required by law and at Customer’s
cost, without exposing other tenants’ data.

## 11. Liability

Liability follows the Terms unless mandatory law says otherwise.

## 12. India DPDP

Where DPDP applies, Provider will implement reasonable security safeguards
and support erasure/grievance as in the privacy policy. Provider is not a
Consent Manager.
