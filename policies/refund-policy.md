## Paid plans

Pro is **USD 12 per mailbox per month** (or USD 99/year if offered). Charged
via PayPal for customers outside India, or UPI/bank invoice in India.

## When we refund

We refund the **first** paid charge if, within **7 days** of that charge,
**all** of the following are true:

1. You ask `[SUPPORT_EMAIL]` within those 7 days, and
2. **We** failed: Tagsmith could not connect to Gmail, did not apply any
   labels, or the product was broken in a way that prevented labeling, and
3. You have not already received a refund on that mailbox.

We will refund on the **same rail** you paid (PayPal refund, or UPI/bank
reversal in India). PayPal’s processing fee is usually **not** returned to
us; we absorb it.

A refund includes **disconnecting Google** and **deleting hosted data** for
that account.

## When we do not refund

- After a **successful sync that applied one or more labels** — the service
  was performed.
- Change of mind, unused Pro time after labeling started, or “I don’t like
  the labels.”
- Chargebacks / PayPal disputes after a successful labeling run (we will
  still refund if **we** were at fault under the 7-day rule).
- Second and later billing periods (cancel instead).

## Cancel

Cancel anytime. You keep Pro until the end of the paid period. **No
pro-rata refund** after labeling has succeeded. We will not charge the
next period.

## How to ask

Email `[SUPPORT_EMAIL]` from the Google account email you used, with the
PayPal / invoice id. We aim to decide within 5 business days.
